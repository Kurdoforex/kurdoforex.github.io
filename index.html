Trading vocabulary

Forex Glossary

Search important trading terminology.

Forex

The foreign exchange market where currencies are traded.

Spread

The difference between the bid and ask price.

Lot

A standardized unit used to describe trading position size.

Leverage

A mechanism that increases market exposure relative to deposited capital and also increases risk.

Stop Loss

An order or planned exit level designed to limit a potential loss.

Take Profit

A planned exit level used to lock in a potential gain.

Liquidity

The availability of buyers and sellers around a price area.

BOS

Break of Structure — a break of a relevant previous structural point.

CHOCH

Change of Character — a term traders use for a possible change in market behavior or structure.

FVG

Fair Value Gap — commonly used to describe a three-candle price imbalance.

Order Block

A price area traders identify as potentially significant based on price action and market structure.

Risk / Reward

A comparison between the amount potentially risked and the amount potentially targeted.
