XAUUSD Core Execution Rules

The SMC concepts taught inside the academy.

1. Liquidity Sweeps

Identify important highs and lows where liquidity may be located before searching for confirmation.

2. CHOCH

Study changes in market structure and wait for confirmation rather than entering only because liquidity was taken.

3. BOS

Use Break of Structure as a framework for analysing potential continuation.

4. Fair Value Gap

Learn how price imbalances form during strong displacement and how they can fit into a larger structure.

5. Order Blocks

Study potential institutional areas of interest and how they interact with liquidity and structure.

6. Risk Management

Every trade should have predefined risk, invalidation and reward parameters before execution.
